ABOUT GOKULAN PRESS

Your Trusted Printing Partner in Gudiyatham

Founded on precision, reliability, and heartfelt dedication to every print, Gokulan Press & Offset is Gudiyatham’s premier destination for complete commercial printing, custom graphic design, and personalized gift solutions.

Located in the heart of Gudiyatham, Vellore District, Tamil Nadu, Gokulan Press & Offset has earned the trust of businesses, families, medical professionals, and institutions across the region by upholding our motto: "Quality That Builds Trust".

From high-volume commercial offset printing and custom-bound bill books to bespoke luxury wedding invitations, vibrant outdoor flex banners, crystal-clear doctor pads, and customized personalized gifting products, we combine modern printing technologies with careful attention to detail.

Whether you require a fresh visiting card to represent your brand, beautiful invitation suites for your family's memorable wedding, or custom photo mugs and frames to gift your loved ones, our team brings creativity, crisp color reproduction, and prompt turnaround to every single project.
